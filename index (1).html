About

Juri
Pranjic.

3D printing entrepreneur based in Switzerland, known online as @3dworkbench. Fan art, functional objects, enthusiast builds and original designs — shared with a community of over 88,000 makers.

From screen-accurate replicas to everyday household objects to custom automotive parts, the work covers a wide range. Everything shared is tested under real conditions, built alongside a full-time job.

The goal has always been the same: share what works and build a community worth being part of.
