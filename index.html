About

Juri Pranjic.

3D printing entrepreneur based in Switzerland, known online as @3dworkbench. Over 85,000 followers and 1,400 published prints spanning fan art, functional objects, enthusiast builds and original designs.

From screen-accurate replicas to everyday household objects to custom automotive parts, the work covers a wide range. Everything shared here is tested under real conditions, built alongside a full-time job.

The goal has always been the same: share what works, skip what does not, and build a community worth being part of.
