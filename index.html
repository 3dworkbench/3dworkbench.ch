About

Juri
Pranjic.

3D printing creator based in Switzerland, known online as @3dworkbench. Fan art, replicas, functional objects and original designs, built across two brands and shared with a growing maker community.

From screen-accurate replicas to everyday household objects to custom automotive parts, the work covers a wide range. Everything is tested under real conditions and built to a high standard.

The goal has always been the same: make good work, collaborate with the right people and build a community worth being part of.
